Peace of Mind for Georgia Policyholders

Welcome to the Georgia Life & Health Insurance Guaranty Association (GLHIGA) website. We hope you find these resources helpful. Our mission is to protect Georgia resident policyholders when a member life or health insurance company becomes insolvent. Explore our site to learn more about the protections, coverage, and consumer resources available to you, and how the guaranty association works to safeguard policyholders during an insurer’s insolvency.

Our Mission:

The Georgia Life & Health Insurance Guaranty Association (GLHIGA) protects Georgia policyholders and beneficiaries by providing a safety net when a licensed life or health insurance company becomes insolvent. Through efficient administration, responsible stewardship of resources, and clear communication, GLHIGA helps ensure eligible covered claims and benefits are paid in accordance with Georgia law while educating consumers about the protections available to them.

Our Vision:

To be a trusted resource for Georgia residents, providing confidence, stability, and understanding when life and health insurance companies become insolvent, while promoting awareness of the protections available under Georgia’s guaranty system.
